📘 Complete YouTube Channel Setup Guide

Follow this master checklist to link your YouTube channel, authorize automated publishing via Google Cloud OAuth, and configure algorithmic upload settings for maximum virality and search visibility.

Step 1Locate Your YouTube Channel ID Open Studio ↗

YouTube requires the raw 24-character Channel ID starting with UC (e.g., UC_x5XG1OV2P6uZZ5FSM9Ttw). Custom handles (like @MrBeast) or vanity URLs are not accepted by the YouTube Data API.

  1. Log in to YouTube Studio.
  2. Click Settings (left gear icon) > Channel > Advanced settings.
  3. Scroll down and click Manage YouTube account or look for Channel ID.
  4. Copy the value that begins with UC....
Step 2Google Cloud OAuth 2.0 Credentials GCP Console ↗

Automated publishing requires an authorized OAuth 2.0 refresh token with upload scopes:

  1. Go to the Google Cloud Console and create or select a project.
  2. Navigate to APIs & Services > Library and enable YouTube Data API v3.
  3. Go to OAuth consent screen, select External, and add your YouTube Google account as a Test user.
  4. Go to Credentials > Create Credentials > OAuth client ID (Type: Desktop app or Web application).
  5. Required Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload.
  6. Place your downloaded client_secrets.json into the storage/ folder or paste your refreshed OAuth token JSON directly in the Channel Setup modal.
At-Rest Encryption: All tokens are transparently encrypted with Fernet symmetric cryptography in storage/shorts.db.
Step 3Category & COPPA Compliance Critical Setting
  • Category ID: The YouTube Shorts algorithmic feed strongly indexes your video category. Recommended:
    • 22: People & Blogs (Universal reach, highest general recommendation)
    • 24: Entertainment (High virality, narrative hooks)
    • 20: Gaming (Gaming clips, walkthroughs, moments)
    • 27: Education (Tutorials, mind-blown facts, tech explanations)
  • COPPA (Made for Kids): Keep this UNCHECKED unless your content is solely directed at young children. Marking a video as "Made for Kids" disables viewer comments, end screens, and notification bells, significantly reducing viral engagement.
Step 4SEO Tags & Peak Upload Timing Algorithm Boost
  • Default Tags: Enter 3–5 broad viral hashtags such as #Shorts, #Viral, #Trending, #FYP. The autonomous SEO engine automatically appends 15 niche-specific high-volume tags during clip synthesis.
  • Peak Publish Hour (UTC): Convert your audience's local peak traffic hours to UTC. For example:
    • 18:00 UTC = 2:00 PM EST / 11:00 AM PST (North America peak)
    • 13:00 UTC = 6:30 PM IST / 6:00 PM PKT (South Asia peak)
  • Drip-Feed Quota: YouTube Data API v3 enforces a default limit of 10,000 quota units/day. Video uploads consume 1,600 units each (~6 uploads/day max per project). Keep daily quota set to 4–5 to avoid quota exhaustion.
